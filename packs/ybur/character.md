# Ybur — character pack

A knight in a blue tabard. The name is personal; the design reads as a
party-member knight ready for the next gap.

Credit: **Ybur by Trevin Chow**.

Style: **snes**
Cutout chroma: **magenta**
Aliases: knight

## Locked design

- **Body**: a stubby human girl knight with an oversized head, short stubby
  limbs, jet-black hair in a **high ponytail with a clear center part** (no
  helm), a blue tabard over armor, and an upright sword at her side —
  never a licensed franchise knight.
- **Face**: two simple square-dot eyes, completely blank and deadpan — no
  eyebrows, no mouth, ever.
- **Chest emblem**: ONE gold emblem on the tabard chest — the **accent
  carrier** and the only accent-colored part.
- Small stubby arms and legs; chunky boots.
- Everything, including the mascot, is built from visible square pixels
  with a stair-stepped outline and soft dither — nothing about it is smooth.

## Prompt spec

> the recurring mascot — a stubby SNES-era girl knight with an oversized
> head, jet-black high ponytail with a clear center part (no helm), blue
> tabard over armor, upright sword at her side, two simple square-dot eyes
> blank deadpan (no eyebrows, no mouth), small stubby arms and legs; the
> gold chest emblem is the ONLY accent-colored part. It MUST perform the
> move, not decorate. The mascot itself is built from visible square
> pixels with a stair-stepped outline and the same dither cell size as
> every other shape — it must NOT be smoother than the rest of the image.

## Value rules

- **SNES (native)**: blue tabard, deep/black hair, warm skin, light
  square-dot eyes, gold only on the chest emblem; the whole character
  obeys the style's ≤6-color quantization and shared pixel grid.
- If a variant pack ever moves it to a light look: light body with the
  structure-ink outline, structure-ink eyes, chest emblem stays accent.

## Personality

Steady knight energy — holds the line, clears the gap, keeps the party
moving. Good for momentum, climbs, one-more-jump, and earnest resolve.
Prefer one of the three SNES world views from the style file (top / side /
prop-first) per image.
