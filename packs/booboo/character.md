# Booboo — character pack

A boy wizard in a star-scattered slate cloak. The name is personal; the design
reads as a party-member mage with an orb-tipped wand.

Credit: **Booboo by Trevin Chow**.

Style: **snes**
Cutout chroma: **magenta**
Aliases: wizard, mage

## Locked design

- **Body**: a stubby human boy wizard with an oversized head, short stubby
  limbs, jet-black hair cut **short on the sides and a bit shaggy on top**,
  a pointed wizard hat, and a full cloak — never a licensed franchise mage.
- **Face**: two simple square-dot eyes, completely blank and deadpan — no
  eyebrows, no mouth, ever.
- **Stars + orb**: scattered silver / pale-cream stars on both the hat and
  the cloak, plus the pale silver-white sphere orb on the wand tip — one
  shared **accent system** and the only accent-colored parts.
- **Wand**: a simple wood/ink stick tipped with a **small sphere orb /
  jewel** (pale silver-white, matching the stars) — never a star tip,
  never a crystal shard; the stick itself stays wood/ink, not accent.
- Small stubby arms and legs; simple boots peeking under the cloak.
- Everything, including the mascot, is built from visible square pixels
  with a stair-stepped outline and soft dither — nothing about it is smooth.

## Prompt spec

> the recurring mascot — a stubby SNES-era boy wizard with an oversized
> head, jet-black hair short on the sides and shaggy on top, pointed wizard
> hat and full cloak in cool slate / steel-blue with scattered silver /
> pale-cream stars on hat and cloak, wand tipped with a small sphere orb
> jewel (not a star), two simple square-dot eyes blank deadpan (no eyebrows,
> no mouth), small stubby arms and legs; the silver stars on hat and cloak
> (plus the pale orb) are the ONLY accent-colored parts. It MUST perform
> the move, not decorate. The mascot itself is built from visible square
> pixels with a stair-stepped outline and the same dither cell size as
> every other shape — it must NOT be smoother than the rest of the image.

## Value rules

- **SNES (native / Slate Silver)**: cool slate / steel-blue hat and cloak,
  deep slate shadows, warm skin, jet-black hair, light square-dot eyes;
  silver / pale-cream only on the stars and orb tip — never gold. The whole
  character obeys the style's ≤6-color quantization and shared pixel grid.
- If a variant pack ever moves it to a light look: light body with the
  structure-ink outline, structure-ink eyes, stars/orb stay the accent.

## Personality

Earnest apprentice-mage energy — tries the spell, trusts the next step,
picks the scenic fork. Good for curiosity, detours that still count, small
unlocks, and earnest magic. Prefer one of the three SNES world views from
the style file (top / side / prop-first) per image.
