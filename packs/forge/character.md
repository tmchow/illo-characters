# Forge — character pack

A smith apprentice with an apron and hammer. The name reads off the work —
forge is where things get made.

Credit: **Forge by Trevin Chow**.

Style: **snes**
Cutout chroma: **magenta**
Aliases: smith, apprentice

## Locked design

- **Body**: a stubby human boy smith apprentice with an oversized head,
  messy short hair, short stubby limbs, a work apron over a tunic, and a
  hammer held at his side — never a knight or scout silhouette.
- **Face**: two simple square-dot eyes, completely blank and deadpan — no
  eyebrows, no mouth, ever.
- **Hammer head**: the gold hammer head — the **accent carrier** and the
  only accent-colored part (apron straps/buckles stay mid or deep, never
  gold).
- Small stubby arms and legs; simple boots.
- Everything, including the mascot, is built from visible square pixels
  with a stair-stepped outline and soft dither — nothing about it is smooth.

## Interaction model

- Contact surfaces: the rounded hand-nubs (press, pat, carry a thing
  against the body — no fingers, no grasp) and the simple boots (stand,
  brace).
- Reach: stubby.
- Grip: pressure/contact only.
- Support/locomotion: feet.
- Protected regions: the face interior — only the two square-dot eyes
  appear there.
- Special operators: the hammer — the one declared held prop, resting
  against a hand-nub at his side; carrying it is design-locked, not
  evidence of general grasp.

> smoother than the rest of the image.

## Value rules

- **SNES (native)**: warm apron, mid-cool tunic, deep hair/boots, light
  square-dot eyes, gold only on the hammer head; the whole character obeys
  the style's ≤6-color quantization and shared pixel grid.
- If a variant pack ever moves it to a light look: light body with the
  structure-ink outline, structure-ink eyes, hammer head stays accent.

## Personality

Apprentice smith energy — makes the tool, fixes the hinge, keeps the forge
warm. Good for craft, iteration, unlocks, and the quiet work of building
the next thing. Prefer one of the three SNES world views from the style
file (top / side / prop-first) per image.
