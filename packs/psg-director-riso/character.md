# PSG Director - character pack

A wide, calm creature who clears a path for others, with a slight smile and a black shirt marked PSG Director.

Credit: **PSG Director by Haim Shrayer**.

Style: **riso**
Cutout chroma: **magenta**

## Locked design

- **Body**: A broad horizontal rounded-rectangle head with soft corners over a wide low torso. The head is about half of the full standing height and roughly twice as wide as it is tall. No visible neck. Soft cream skin; no ears, hair, horns, antenna, tail, or fur.
- **Face**: Two dark circular dot eyes, two short curved eyebrows with the character's right brow (viewer-left in a front view) slightly higher, and one small asymmetric closed smile rising toward the character's left (viewer-right in a front view). No nose, cheeks, eyelashes, teeth, or tongue. Keep these exact marks and their expression in every scene.
- **Limbs**: two short rounded arms with smooth tips, no separate fingers or thumbs; two short legs ending in rounded bare feet.
- **Shirt**: one plain matte black short-sleeve T-shirt. Centered serif chest lettering reads exactly "PSG" on the first line and "Director" on the second line.
- **Accent carrier**: the shirt's single chest-lettering area, printed in gold. This is the only accent-colored part; the shirt fabric itself is neutral black.
- No extra garments, shoes, logos, badges, tools attached to the body, or new facial details. Scene props stay separate from the design.
- Keep the reference proportions. A pose may tilt the body but must not stretch the head or lengthen the limbs.

## Interaction model

- Contact surfaces: rounded arm tips press, push, brace, or support a nearby object from below; feet stand, step, and brace.
- Reach: stubby; contacts remain close beside or below the torso.
- Grip: pressure/contact only. No fingers, pinching, or handles held in a fist.
- Support/locomotion: two feet.
- Protected regions: the face holds only its locked marks; chest lettering stays legible when the front of the shirt is visible.

## Value rules

- **All palettes**: cream body, dark face marks, matte black shirt, and gold lettering. The default gold is #D4AF57. Treat the lettering as one printed chest area; no other gold marks.
- **Light grounds**: preserve the cream body with a clear dark contour and keep the shirt lettering readable.
- **Dark grounds**: keep the body light and use a pale neutral edge where needed to separate the black shirt from the scene. Do not turn the skin or shirt gold.
- **Riso treatment**: one bold, even, softly rounded charcoal outline, flat cream and black fills, subtle halftone grain, and warm paper texture. Keep text and silhouette registration clear; no pencil hatching or smooth gradients.

## Personality

A broad creature who sees where things need to go and clears the way. Calm, quietly amused, and firm when something blocks progress. The raised brow and slight smile stay fixed; the body does the serious work.
