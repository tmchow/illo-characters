# PSG Manager - character pack

A tall oval creature who keeps a wobbling load steady, with a small smile and a black shirt marked PSG Manager.

Credit: **PSG Manager by Haim Shrayer**.

Style: **sketchbook**
Cutout chroma: **magenta**

## Locked design

- **Body**: A tall vertical oval head over a narrow oval torso. The head is about three fifths of the full standing height and roughly two thirds as wide as it is tall. No visible neck. Soft cream skin; no ears, hair, horns, antenna, tail, or fur.
- **Face**: Two dark circular dot eyes, two short gently curved eyebrows at equal height, and one small symmetrical closed curved smile. No nose, cheeks, eyelashes, teeth, or tongue. Keep these exact marks and their expression in every scene.
- **Limbs**: two short rounded arms with smooth tips, no separate fingers or thumbs; two short legs ending in rounded bare feet.
- **Shirt**: one plain matte black short-sleeve T-shirt. Centered serif chest lettering reads exactly "PSG" on the first line and "Manager" on the second line.
- **Accent carrier**: the shirt's single chest-lettering area, printed in gold. This is the only accent-colored part; the shirt fabric itself is neutral black.
- No extra garments, shoes, logos, badges, tools attached to the body, or new facial details. Scene props stay separate from the design.
- Keep the reference proportions. A pose may tilt the body but must not stretch the head or lengthen the limbs.

## Interaction model

- Contact surfaces: rounded arm tips press, push, brace, or support a nearby object from below; feet stand, step, and brace.
- Reach: stubby; contacts remain close beside or below the torso.
- Grip: pressure/contact only. No fingers, pinching, or handles held in a fist.
- Support/locomotion: two feet.
- Protected regions: the face holds only its locked marks; chest lettering stays legible when the front of the shirt is visible.

## Value rules

- **All palettes**: cream body, dark face marks, matte black shirt, and gold lettering. The default gold is #D4AF57. Treat the lettering as one printed chest area; no other gold marks.
- **Light grounds**: preserve the cream body with a clear dark contour and keep the shirt lettering readable.
- **Dark grounds**: keep the body light and use a pale neutral edge where needed to separate the black shirt from the scene. Do not turn the skin or shirt gold.
- **Sketchbook treatment**: warm sepia pencil-and-ink contours with fine cross-hatching on body edges and props; dense charcoal texture on the black shirt. Keep the face clear. Warm paper grain, no glossy shading. The approved gold lettering deliberately replaces the look's usual cool accent.

## Personality

A steady oval creature who makes space for the next step and keeps a busy day in order. Friendly, attentive, and ready to support a load before it falls. The smile stays small even when the work is serious.
