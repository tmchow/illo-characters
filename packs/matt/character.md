# Matt — character pack

A hooded scout who checks the path ahead. The name is personal; the design
reads as a party-member scout on the overworld.

Credit: **Matt by Trevin Chow**.

Style: **snes**
Cutout chroma: **magenta**
Aliases: scout

## Locked design

- **Body**: a stubby human scout with an oversized head, short stubby limbs,
  a pointed hood/capelet over a mid-cool tunic, and a short cape behind —
  never Mario, Link, or other licensed silhouettes.
- **Face**: two simple square-dot eyes, completely blank and deadpan — no
  eyebrows, no mouth, ever.
- **Hood lining**: the thin gold inner rim of the hood — the **accent
  carrier** and the only accent-colored part.
- Small stubby arms and legs; simple boots.
- Everything, including the mascot, is built from visible square pixels
  with a stair-stepped outline and soft dither — nothing about it is smooth.

## Interaction model

- Contact surfaces: the stubby arm tips — press, push, pat, and carry
  only, no grasp (the pixel mitts have no fingers); the boots stand and
  step.
- Reach: stubby.
- Grip: pressure/contact only.
- Support/locomotion: feet — the stubby booted legs.
- Protected regions: the face interior — only the two square-dot eyes
  appear there.

## Prompt spec

> the recurring mascot — a stubby SNES-era human scout with an oversized
> head, pointed hood/capelet over a mid-cool tunic, short cape behind, two
> simple square-dot eyes blank deadpan (no eyebrows, no mouth), small stubby
> arms and legs; the thin gold hood lining is the ONLY accent-colored part.
> It MUST perform the move, not decorate. The mascot itself is built from
> visible square pixels with a stair-stepped outline and the same dither
> cell size as every other shape — it must NOT be smoother than the rest of
> the image.

## Value rules

- **SNES (native)**: mid-cool hood/tunic, deep cape, warm skin, light
  square-dot eyes on the face, gold only on the hood lining; the whole
  character obeys the style's ≤6-color quantization and shared pixel grid.
- If a variant pack ever moves it to a light look: light body with the
  structure-ink outline, structure-ink eyes, hood lining stays accent.

## Personality

Earnest scout energy — maps routes, checks the next tile, keeps moving.
Good for journeys, exploration, getting unstuck on the map, and the quiet
work of scouting ahead. Prefer one of the three SNES world views from the
style file (top / side / prop-first) per image.
