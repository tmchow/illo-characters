# Agent Waffle — character pack

Agent Waffle is the helpful AI agent mascot — a square waffle with golden syrup that guides users through tasks and keeps systems running smoothly.

Credit: **Agent Waffle by Wintermute**.

Style: **blueprint**
Cutout chroma: **magenta**

## Locked design

- **Body**: a simple square waffle silhouette with the classic grid pattern — solid blueprint lines forming the waffle squares, with stubby arms and legs.
- **Face**: two dot eyes left as uncarved paper, completely blank and deadpan — no eyebrows, no mouth, ever.
- **Syrup**: the **accent carrier** — the golden syrup dripping from the melting butter pat on top prints in the accent ink, slightly off-register. The only accent-colored part.
- Butter pat: a square pat of butter on top, rendered as solid blueprint ink.
- Never: expressive arms (beyond stubby), legs (beyond stubby), smile, rosy cheeks, or additional facial features.

## Prompt spec

> the helpful AI agent mascot — a simple square waffle silhouette with classic grid pattern rendered as solid blueprint ink with two dot eyes left as uncarved paper, blank deadpan (no eyebrows, no mouth), stubby arms and legs, square butter pat on top; the golden syrup dripping from the butter pat is the ONLY accent-colored part. It MUST perform the move, not decorate.

## Value rules

- **Blueprint (native)**: body is solid blueprint ink forming the waffle grid with paper eyes and stubby limbs; the syrup drips in the accent ink, slightly off-register.
- If a variant pack ever moves it to a dark look: dark body with thick blueprint outline, ink eyes, syrup stays accent.
- If a variant pack ever moves it to a light look: light body with thin blueprint outline, ink eyes, syrup stays accent.

## Personality

Reliable and steadfast — it keeps the syrup flowing and the grids aligned, ready to assist with any task that needs doing.
